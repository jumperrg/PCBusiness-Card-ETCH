Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

A focused tool built for the one thing: PCB business cards. No bloat, no subscriptions, no learning curve.

Runs in the Browser

No install, no account. Open the editor and start designing immediately. Your data never leaves your machine.

7 Board Colors

Choose from authentic solder mask colors — green, black, white, blue, red, yellow, purple — matched to real PCB fab samples.

Trace Routing

Draw copper traces, place vias and pads just like a real EDA tool. Snap to grid, bend at 45°, keep it clean.

Export to SVG & Gerber

Download production-ready files and upload directly to JLCPCB, PCBWay, or any fab house. Order 5 cards for a few dollars.

Silkscreen Text

Add your name, email, handle, QR code — whatever fits on a standard 85 × 54 mm business card format.

Gerber Preview

See exactly what your board will look like before ordering. Copper, silkscreen, and board outline rendered accurately.
